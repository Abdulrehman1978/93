> **Topic:** The Closed-Loop Service Delivery Pipeline: From Risk Detection to Verified Support  
> **Core Principle:** "Stay Until Support Arrives" — Solving the Broken Referral Chasm.

## 1. The Closed-Loop Referral Lifecycle

```text
[ DETECTED & RECOMMENDED ]
├── Automated recommendation generated by SupportRecommendationEngine
└── Matched against verified resource directory (jurisdiction, language, category)
       │
       ▼
[ OPERATOR REVIEW & APPROVAL ]
├── Operator inspects recommendation and evidence in Live Copilot
├── Complainant provides specific consent to share information ("Share Less" principle)
└── Transition: Operator clicks [APPROVE & DISPATCH]
       │
       ▼
[ REFERRED (DISPATCHED) ]
├── Safe Handoff Package encrypted and transmitted via agency adapter
├── Bi-directional webhook listener registered; SMS confirmation sent to victim
└── SLA Timer Initialized:
    • Critical Priority: Acknowledgement SLA = 2 Hours
    • High Priority: Acknowledgement SLA = 12 Hours
    • Moderate Priority: Acknowledgement SLA = 48 Hours
       │
       ▼
[ ACKNOWLEDGED BY AGENCY ]
├── Receiving agency (Tele-MANAS, DLSA, OSC) confirms receipt via webhook / SMS
├── Assigned caseworker / counselor / advocate ID returned to SAMBAL
└── IF SLA BREACH: System triggers automated Supervisor Escalation Alert
       │
       ▼
[ CONTACT ESTABLISHED ]
├── Agency caseworker contacts victim via consented channel
└── Logs contact timestamp in SAMBAL via bi-directional callback
       │
       ▼
[ APPOINTMENT SCHEDULED / SERVICE STARTED ]
├── Tele-counseling session held, legal aid consultation conducted, or shelter provided
└── Status updated in citizen PWA Case Tracker (non-stigmatizing progress bar)
       │
       ▼
[ FOLLOW-UP CADENCE (7-Day & 14-Day Automated Triggers) ]
├── Automated follow-up task queued for 14566 welfare operator
├── Operator verifies: "Did you receive the assistance? Do you feel safer now?"
└── Complainant confirms support experience and current safety state
       │
       ▼
[ VERIFIED SUPPORT OUTCOME LOGGED ]
├── Outcome recorded: SUPPORT_DELIVERED_SUCCESSFULLY | ONGOING_ENGAGEMENT | ESCALATED
└── Closed-loop record archived into Ministry Outcome Intelligence database
```

## 2. Safe Handoff & "Share Less" Minimization

To prevent re-traumatizing the victim while upholding DPDP Act data minimization, the **Safe Handoff Package** enforces strict redaction boundaries:

### What IS Consented & Shared:
- Complainant preferred language and safe contact phone/channel.
- General incident category (e.g. *Atrocity Offence - Land Coercion and Physical Assault*).
- Current safety status (*Immediate danger cleared / Shelter requested*).
- High-level emotional distress indicators (*Elevated trauma distress, sleep disruption*).
- Jurisdictional police station and docket ID.

### What is STRICTLY NOT Shared:
- Raw audio voice recordings.
- Detailed trauma narrative transcripts not required for the specific service.
- Internal AI model confidence diagnostics or SVI raw scores.
- Unrelated legal allegations (kept strictly within police/legal jurisdiction).
- Contact details of non-consenting family members.

## 3. "Never-Repeat-My-Story" Memory Layer

The platform enforces provenance tagging on all case intelligence to ensure that whenever a counselor, lawyer, or supervisor opens the file, they immediately know the origin of every fact:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [ COMPLAINANT REPORTED ] (Original Voice Transcript)   │
│ "They threw stones at our door and broke our window."  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ AI EXTRACTED ] (Extracted Entity)                    │
│ Property Damage: Residence | Perpetrator: Mob          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ OPERATOR CONFIRMED ] (Human Verification)            │
│ Verified: Incident occurred on night of 2026-10-02     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ COUNSELLOR REPORTED ] (Support Update)               │
│ Tele-MANAS Session 1 completed: Coping exercises given │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
This architecture prevents the fragmentation that plagues Indian public helplines, ensuring that every touchpoint builds upon previously established facts.
