## 1. Assumptions Register Table

| Assumption ID | Domain | Statement of Assumption | Operational Justification | Failure Risk & Impact | Architectural Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `ASM-01` | **Statutory Scope** | Complainants contacting 14566 are primarily seeking justice, protection, and relief under the PCR Act 1955 and PoA Act 1989. | 14566 is the dedicated statutory helpline launched by MoSJE specifically for anti-atrocity enforcement. | Complainants may report unrelated civil or non-atrocity disputes, skewing SVI scoring. | System implements domain qualification taxonomy during intake; non-atrocity grievances routed to standard CPGRAMS/general queues. |
| `ASM-02` | **Telephony Infrastructure** | Incoming telephone calls to 14566 arrive via standard PSTN / VoIP trunks providing 8kHz or 16kHz audio streams. | Standard telecom service provider trunking across Indian BSNL/MTNL/Jio/Airtel networks. | 8kHz audio has band cutoff above 4kHz, stripping higher formant frequencies needed for subtle prosody. | Audio pipeline includes pre-processing band-pass normalization; DSP acoustic models benchmarked explicitly on downsampled 8kHz telephone speech. |
| `ASM-03` | **Linguistic Diversity** | While India possesses 22 scheduled languages, the overwhelming majority of 14566 calls in high-atrocity states occur in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and Indian English. | NCRB (National Crime Records Bureau) atrocity incidence data identifies UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu as high-volume zones. | Complainants speaking unrepresented tribal dialects (e.g., Gondi, Santhali) may face low ASR accuracy. | System supports manual operator transcript editing and human translation fallback; dialect uncertainty directly widens downstream SVI confidence bands. |
| `ASM-04` | **DPDP Act Conformance** | The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 permits processing of sensitive personal data for emergency response and statutory public duties under explicit consent. | Section 7(b) and Section 7(g) of DPDP Act 2023 cover state statutory services and medical/safety emergencies. | Legal challenge over consent revocation or cross-agency referral data sharing. | Granular consent model separating transcription, audio processing, and external referral sharing; "Share Less" selective data transmission implemented. |
| `ASM-05` | **Audio Retention Policy** | Complainants do not wish their voice recordings to be permanently archived unless legally required for criminal evidence. | Trauma-informed dignity and victim safety require minimization of biometric and voiceprint leakage. | Loss of training data for acoustic model iteration. | Ephemeral audio policy: audio streamed, DSP features and transcript extracted, raw audio deleted after session unless explicit complainant consent is granted. |
| `ASM-06` | **Government API Availability** | Neither MoSJE nor Tele-MANAS (MoHFW) currently exposes an open, public, unauthenticated external REST API for hackathon participants. | Verified empirical research of current NIC, MoHFW, and MoSJE digital infrastructure. | Disqualifying the project if judges believe we fabricated a live government link. | Clean Adapter pattern implemented: `SANDBOX` mode provides realistic simulation with error injection; `ADAPTER_READY` provides typed OpenAPI 3.1 contracts. |
| `ASM-07` | **Operator Authority** | An AI module must never independently dispatch police or make unilateral legal decisions without human operator confirmation. | Article 21 constitutional due process and public administration ethics in high-stakes governance. | Unintended police deployment to a complainant's residence could escalate domestic or caste violence. | Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) mandatory: AI acts purely as decision support; every referral or dispatch requires human authorization. |
| `ASM-08` | **Offline / Degraded Realities** | District-level helpline call centers or mobile operators in rural areas frequently experience cloud or fiber internet disruptions. | Indian public sector tier-2/tier-3 infrastructure realities. | Complete system lockout during network downtime if reliant entirely on cloud LLMs. | Hybrid architecture: Local ASR (`faster-whisper`), local SQLite/PostgreSQL, and deterministic regex safety rules run 100% offline without internet. |
