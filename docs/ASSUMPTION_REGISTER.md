## 1. Assumptions Register Table

| Assumption ID | Domain | Statement of Assumption | Operational Justification | Failure Risk & Impact | Architectural Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `ASM-01` | **Statutory Scope** | Complainants contacting 14566 are primarily seeking justice, protection, and relief under the PCR Act 1955 and PoA Act 1989. | 14566 is the dedicated statutory helpline launched by MoSJE specifically for anti-atrocity enforcement. | Complainants may report unrelated civil or non-atrocity disputes, skewing SVI scoring. | System implements domain qualification taxonomy during intake; non-atrocity grievances routed to standard CPGRAMS/general queues. |
| `ASM-02` | **Telephony Infrastructure** | Incoming telephone calls to 14566 arrive via standard PSTN / VoIP trunks providing 8kHz or 16kHz audio streams. | Standard telecom service provider trunking across Indian BSNL/MTNL/Jio/Airtel networks. | 8kHz audio has band cutoff above 4kHz, stripping higher formant frequencies needed for subtle prosody. | Audio pipeline includes pre-processing band-pass normalization; DSP acoustic models benchmarked explicitly on downsampled 8kHz telephone speech. |
| `ASM-03` | **Linguistic Diversity** | While India possesses 22 scheduled languages, the overwhelming majority of 14566 calls in high-atrocity states occur in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and Indian English. | NCRB (National Crime Records Bureau) atrocity incidence data identifies UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu as high-volume zones. | Complainants speaking unrepresented tribal dialects (e.g., Gondi, Santhali) may face low ASR accuracy. | System supports manual operator transcript editing and human translation fallback; dialect uncertainty directly widens downstream SVI confidence bands. |
| `ASM-04` | **Phased DPDP Framework & Lawful Basis** | DPDP Act 2023 operates under phased commencement (13 Nov 2025 notification; substantive operational sections commence May 2027). Public welfare and emergency life-safety actions rest on distinct lawful bases (`STATE_FUNCTION_UNDER_LAW`, `MEDICAL_EMERGENCY`) and do not deadlock on consent. | Official MeitY Gazette notification S.O. 5042(E). Public welfare delivery cannot legally require individual consent for sovereign grievance redressal. | Conflating lawful bases with commercial consent could paralyze emergency response or violate statutory duties. | System separates `LawfulBasis` from `ConsentRecord`. Privacy-by-design baseline implemented in advance of statutory enforcement (`DPDP_READY`). |
| `ASM-05` | **Audio Retention Policy** | Complainants do not wish their voice recordings to be permanently archived; retaining raw audio creates biometric surveillance risks. | Trauma-informed dignity and victim safety require minimization of biometric and voiceprint leakage. | Loss of training data for acoustic model iteration. | Ephemeral audio policy: audio streamed, DSP features and transcript extracted, raw audio deleted from RAM immediately unless explicit complainant consent is granted. Multi-stage deletion tracking implemented. |
| `ASM-06` | **Government API Availability** | Neither MoSJE nor Tele-MANAS (MoHFW) currently exposes an open, public, unauthenticated external REST API for hackathon participants. | Verified empirical research of current NIC, MoHFW, and MoSJE digital infrastructure. | Disqualifying the project if judges believe we fabricated a live government link. | Clean Adapter pattern implemented: `SANDBOX` mode provides realistic simulation with error injection; `ADAPTER_READY` provides typed OpenAPI 3.1 contracts. |
| `ASM-07` | **Operator Authority & Emergency Boundary** | `ERSS112Adapter` / police / protection integrations are strictly human-authorized handoff boundaries. An AI score or model event may recommend escalation but may NOT autonomously contact law enforcement or emergency services unless a future formally approved policy explicitly authorizes it. | Article 21 constitutional due process and public administration ethics in high-stakes governance. | Unintended police deployment to a complainant's residence could escalate domestic or caste violence. | Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) mandatory: AI acts purely as decision support; every referral or dispatch requires human operator authorization. Recorded in `RESPONSIBLE_AI.md`, `INTEGRATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `SAFETY_ENGINE.md`. |
| `ASM-08` | **Offline / Degraded Realities** | District-level helpline call centers or mobile operators in rural areas frequently experience cloud or fiber internet disruptions. | Indian public sector tier-2/tier-3 infrastructure realities. | Complete system lockout during network downtime if reliant entirely on cloud LLMs. | Hybrid architecture: Local ASR baseline, local PostgreSQL instance, and deterministic regex safety rules run 100% offline without internet. |
| `ASM-09` | **Canonical PostgreSQL** | PostgreSQL 16+ is the authoritative, canonical database for development integration, migrations, database testing, E2E, CI, staging, and production. SQLite is not a production alternative. | Ensuring production ACID guarantees, check constraints, JSONB operators, and concurrency isolation (SKIP LOCKED). | Schema or constraint drift if SQLite quirks diverge from PostgreSQL. | All database validations, integration tests, and migrations run against canonical PostgreSQL. SQLite restricted solely to deliberately DB-agnostic unit tests. |
| `ASM-10` | **ASR Baseline Candidate** | `faster-whisper-turbo` (CTranslate2 INT8) is the initial baseline candidate for local development, not a finalized production selection or guaranteed sub-second engine. | Proven local CPU inference capability for initial scaffolding. | Potential accuracy or latency deficits on regional dialects and noisy telephone audio. | Mandatory Packet 08 benchmarking against Indic-focused/government alternatives across WER, latency, and critical-phrase recall before production lock. |
| `ASM-11` | **Non-Diagnostic System Role** | SAMBAL is an administrative and triage support layer, NOT a clinical psychiatric or judicial diagnostic instrument. | Medical licensing laws and statutory criminal procedure mandates. | Misguided clinical labeling of victims or improper legal guilt determinations. | System explicitly bans psychiatric diagnoses and legal guilt determinations across contracts and documentation. |
| `ASM-12` | **Provisional SVI Formulation** | SVI represents a qualitative triage construct; no finalized mathematical formula or static weights exist prior to Packet 11 empirical evaluation. | Responsible AI and avoiding premature algorithmic lock-in. | Encoding biased or unverified weights into early database schemas. | SVI defined strictly conceptually in Packet 02; mathematical fusion deferred to Packet 11. Shared evidence contract strips all additive weights. |
| `ASM-13` | **Verified Support Mandate** | Recommending or transmitting a referral does not equate to delivered support; outcome verification requires tangible evidence. | Real-world attrition where referrals are lost in bureaucratic handoffs. | Misleading leadership with vanity metrics of "successful referrals". | 15-state referral state machine and 7-tier Verified Support hierarchy implemented. |
| `ASM-14` | **Multi-Tier Outcome Verification** | While dual confirmation (`DUAL_CONFIRMED`) is the gold standard, operational referral completion must accommodate cases where citizens cannot be safely contacted, decline calls, or where formal court/police documentation verifies delivery. | Real-world victim safety, trauma avoidance, and administrative closure needs. | Referral records remaining permanently uncloseable in the database. | Separation of referral state from outcome evidence (`SupportOutcomeEvidence`); `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` explicitly modeled without implying service failure. |
| `ASM-15` | **Verifiable Deletion Tracking** | Distributed object stores cannot guarantee instantaneous single-cycle block erasure across geographic replicas and backup snapshots. | Distributed storage architecture and disaster recovery rotation realities. | Making technically false claims of immediate physical block erasure to citizens and auditors. | Implementation of multi-stage deletion lifecycle (`DELETION_REQUESTED` → `PRIMARY_OBJECT_DELETED` → `BACKUP_EXPIRY_PENDING` → `DELETION_VERIFIED`). |
