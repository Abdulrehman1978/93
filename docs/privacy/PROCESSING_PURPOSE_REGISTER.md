## 1. Governance Principles

1. **Lawful Basis Distinct from Consent:** Consent is one of several lawful bases recognized in Indian privacy law and administrative jurisprudence. State welfare delivery, legal obligations, and emergency life-safety actions must not be mischaracterized as "consent-driven," nor allowed to deadlock on consent when a statutory or emergency lawful basis applies.
2. **No Autonomous AI Emergency Overrides:** Under no circumstances may an AI model or algorithmic score invoke an emergency exception or override a user's consent preference. Any emergency processing under `MEDICAL_EMERGENCY` or `PUBLIC_ORDER_OR_DISASTER_ASSISTANCE` requires an authenticated human operator or supervisor decision following documented standard operating procedures.
3. **Strict Data Minimization:** Each processing purpose is restricted to the absolute minimum necessary data categories. No data is collected or retained on speculative future utility.
4. **Transparent Retention Attribution:** Every retention rule must be grounded in an authoritative source. Where statutory retention is unfinalized, it is designated `RETENTION_POLICY_PENDING` rather than invented.
5. **Rigorous Anonymization & Retention Standards:** De-identification is governed by formal risk states (`IDENTIFIABLE`, `PSEUDONYMIZED`, `DE_IDENTIFIED`, `ANONYMIZED_VALIDATED`, `SYNTHETIC`). Datasets are classified as `ANONYMIZED_VALIDATED` only after documented risk assessment. Bounded 24-month retention strictly mandates either deletion or formal versioned extension (archival is retention).

## 4. Consent Lifecycle, Re-Identification Risk & Revocation Mechanics

1. **Pre-Transformation State:** Where consent is the lawful basis, the citizen may revoke consent via citizen portal, SMS, or operator phone request while data is `IDENTIFIABLE` or `PSEUDONYMIZED`. All associated active processing immediately ceases, and data is queued for verified multi-stage deletion (`DELETION_REQUESTED` → `PRIMARY_OBJECT_DELETED` → `DELETION_VERIFIED`).
2. **Post-Validation State (`ANONYMIZED_VALIDATED`):** For research datasets (`PURP-16`), data enters this state only after a documented re-identification risk evaluation confirms that identification is not reasonably possible for the defined threat model and auxiliary datasets. Once validated, individual records cannot be singled out or extracted. Notice informs the user of this technical boundary prior to consent.
3. **Strict Bounded Retention (No Indefinite Archival):** Archival is retention. Moving datasets to cold storage or air-gapped vaults does not bypass retention schedules. At the end of the 24-month period, the system requires either `DELETE` or a `FORMALLY APPROVED VERSIONED EXTENSION` with documented justification, approver ID, and audit logging.
4. **Audit Ledger Independence:** The audit trail of the consent decision itself (`consent_id`, pseudonymous subject reference, timestamp, channel, policy version) is preserved under `LEGAL_OBLIGATION` to prove compliance, even after the underlying personal data is deleted.
